# Asset licenses

## Squirrel artwork

`squirrel.png`, `squirrel.ico`, `squirrel.icns`, `squirrel-body.png`, and
`squirrel-scent.png` are the sniffler squirrel, owned by this project. The two
`squirrel-*.png` files are the same line art tinted navy and split at the nose so
the scent lines can move on their own; the drawing itself is unchanged.

## Icons

The files in `icons/` are Tabler Icons 3.46.0, outline set, unchanged.
Source: https://tabler.io/icons
